Before you buy

Visit a house we finished five years ago.

Ask to see a building from our rental portfolio: the same construction, the same finishes, five years of Accra weather and tenants on them. We would rather you checked than trusted.

Buying from us

Four steps, none of them a surprise.

The purchase pack — permit, title position, payment schedule, inclusions and defects period — is published on every listing.

  1. 01

    Reserve

    A reservation fee holds the unit for an agreed period. The receipt says what it is deducted from.

  2. 02

    Pay against stages

    Foundation, roof, finishes, handover. You pay when a stage is signed off, not on a calendar date.

  3. 03

    Watch it rise

    A dated photograph and the stage percentage every month, whether you ask for it or not.

  4. 04

    Handover

    The snagging list is closed before the keys; the defects period is in writing, not in conversation.
